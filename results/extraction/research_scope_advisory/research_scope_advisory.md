- Surgery type and technique (on-pump vs off-pump; CABG vs valve vs aortic; elective vs urgent/emergent)
- AKI definition and assessment window (48 h vs 120 h vs 7 days vs 1 year)
- Use of urine-output criteria vs creatinine-only
- Study design (blinded RCT vs open-label RCT vs PSM/weighted observational)
- Effect measure and adjustment (RR vs OR; crude vs adjusted; matching vs weighting)
- Baseline renal function and comorbidity profiles (CKD, diabetes prevalence, LVEF)
- Perioperative SGLT2i management (withhold timing, resume timing)
