SCR_000024.pdf reports the Snel et al. trial's combined KDIGO AKI result (creatinine and/or urine output: 5/25 vs 20/30), which was not selected because the creatinine-only result was explicitly provided in the supplementary DOCX. The PDF and DOCX represent the same study and must not be entered as independent contributions.
SCR_000098.pdf (DAPA-CS) reports KDIGO AKI incidence of 40.7% versus 41.0% (RR 0.99, 95% CI 0.69-1.44), but the supplied report does not isolate serum-creatinine-only AKI from any urine-output component. It was therefore excluded from the creatinine-only sensitivity analysis.
The POST-CABGDM results narrative on page 4 gives RR 0.58, while the abstract, graphical summary, and Table 2 give RR 0.57 for the same 16/71 versus 29/74 comparison. RR 0.57 was extracted from the formal outcome table; raw counts are unaffected.
