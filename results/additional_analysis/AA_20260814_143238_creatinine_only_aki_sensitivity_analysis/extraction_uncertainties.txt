SCR_000024.pdf reports combined KDIGO AKI as 5/25 versus 20/30, incorporating creatinine and urine output. These combined data were not used because the creatinine-only endpoint was explicitly isolated in Supplementary Table 3 of SCR_000024의 ScienceDirect Supplementary Tables.docx as 3/25 versus 8/30.
SCR_000098.pdf (DAPA-CS) reports KDIGO AKI incidence of 40.7% versus 41.0% but does not state whether the endpoint used serum creatinine alone or combined creatinine/urine-output criteria, and no creatinine-only event counts are reported. It was therefore excluded rather than assuming the operational definition.
