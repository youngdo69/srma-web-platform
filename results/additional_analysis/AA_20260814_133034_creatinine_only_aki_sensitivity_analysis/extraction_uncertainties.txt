SCR_000024.pdf (Snel et al. open-label randomized pilot study): the main article reports combined KDIGO AKI as 5/25 versus 20/30 and states that creatinine-only AKI incidence was 27% in the control group, but it does not explicitly report an isolatable creatinine-only event count or incidence for the empagliflozin group. The required arm-level endpoint is referred to Supplementary Table 3, which is not contained in the supplied file; therefore it was not included.
SCR_000098.pdf (DAPA-CS): AKI was reported as 40.7% versus 41.0% using KDIGO criteria, but the supplied article does not state that this endpoint was serum-creatinine-only or separately report creatinine-only AKI. It was excluded because the requested endpoint could not be isolated.
