## Revision boundaries

The editor supports plain-prose updates in eligible sections, with comparison, revision reason and immutable manuscript ancestry. Complex inline runs, equations, citation renumbering, and full structured Methods changes remain limited. Unsupported edits preserve the existing approved original. A genuine change to a method follows the separate approved Analysis workflow and requires affected QC/source consistency to be restored.
