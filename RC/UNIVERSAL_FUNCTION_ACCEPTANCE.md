## Scientific workflow and protection contracts

- Final inclusion flows from the approved Human selection snapshot. Later consumers resolve the exact Evidence version and Study IDs. Selection remains distinct from synthesis participation. PRISMA assembly reconciles the final count against registered selection, so a stale R/24/R/35 artifact cannot replace the authoritative selection.
- Approved outcome definitions, labels, and reported timepoints are retained alongside their harmonized assignments and source links. Unreported values are not substituted for observations. AI excerpts remain suggestions within their validated source scope and require the applicable Human decision.
- Source bindings connect project, approved Protocol, Evidence, Analysis, R/38 and R/39, Manuscript and DOCX lineage. R/40 uses exact identifiers, dependency hashes and ruleset identity for invalidation or approved receipt reuse. Worker attempts preserve prior records and inspect only bound inputs.
- The final gate verifies required checks, exact-version author approval and approved DOCX bytes. The Phase C isolated positive scenario used synthetic external acceptance receipts. Negative cases covered missing or failed QC, wrong version or hash, missing author, invalidated QC and renderer failure.
- The authenticated service checks request fields, project permissions and CSRF. Jobs use attempt fences, replay rules and immutable audit. AI semantic execution is separately controlled by a version- and input-bound authorization, cost reservation and retry journal. No provider use was part of this RC verification.

No reproducible P0 universal defect was identified in the audited paths. See [RELEASE_NOTES.md](RELEASE_NOTES.md) for the PM evaluation scope and the requirements for a production release.
