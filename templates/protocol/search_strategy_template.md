## 2. Review question

### Population

Adults undergoing cardiac surgery, including:

* Coronary artery bypass grafting
* Valve surgery
* Aortic surgery
* Combined cardiac surgery
* Other procedures requiring cardiac surgery

### Intervention or exposure

Treatment with a sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitor, including:

* Dapagliflozin
* Empagliflozin
* Canagliflozin
* Ertugliflozin
* SGLT2 inhibitor
* Sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitor

### Comparator

* No SGLT2 inhibitor
* Placebo
* Standard medical treatment
* Other glucose-lowering treatment

### Outcomes

The search will not initially be restricted by outcome terms unless the unrestricted search produces an unmanageable number of records.

Potential outcomes include:

* Acute kidney injury
* Mortality
* Major adverse cardiovascular events
* Heart failure
* Postoperative atrial fibrillation
* Renal replacement therapy
* Length of ICU stay
* Length of hospital stay
* Ketoacidosis
* Hypoglycemia

## 4. Concept combination

The primary search structure is:

```text
Concept 1: SGLT2 inhibitors
AND
Concept 2: Cardiac surgery
```

An optional restricted search structure is:

```text
Concept 1: SGLT2 inhibitors
AND
Concept 2: Cardiac surgery
AND
Concept 3: Perioperative setting
```

Outcome terms should not be included in the primary search unless justified because outcome restrictions may reduce sensitivity.

## 12. Search peer review

The final search strategy should be checked for:

* Correct use of controlled vocabulary
* Appropriate spelling variants
* Drug names and class terms
* Cardiac-surgery synonyms
* Correct Boolean operators
* Correct parentheses
* Correct database field tags
* Avoidance of unnecessarily restrictive outcome terms
* Correct adaptation to each database platform

A PRESS-style peer review may be performed when possible.

---

## 13. Search-update policy

The search will be rerun before manuscript submission when the interval between the initial search and submission is substantial.

All updated records will undergo the same deduplication and screening procedures as the original search.
